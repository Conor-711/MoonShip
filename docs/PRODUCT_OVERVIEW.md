# MoonShip product overview

MoonShip connects capital influence, economic relationships and discovery.

A person sees an idea from another person, chooses that person as the source for a trade and confirms the action. Accepted settlement creates an economic relationship and a separate subject–asset Relation Token. A post remains part of its author's relationships while retaining its own transaction statistics.

## Capital influence and relationships

Profiles expose four complementary views:

| Metric | Product meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Capital Moved | Settled capital that others traded through this person |
| Capital Sourced | Settled capital this person traded through others |
| Capital Followers | Distinct trading subjects who completed trades through this person |
| Capital Following | Distinct sources this person selected for completed trades |

The personal economic graph puts the current person at the center. Users can switch to their Relation Token view. These displays describe recorded economic activity, rather than a social follow action.

## Discovering people and information

The web Discover workspace combines a feed with discovery views for people and posts. Native posts and X posts share a card format. Native posts enter the feed through the posting flow; X posts enter through settled, post-attributed trades. Per-post volume, traders and trades describe that post's accepted economic activity. Author pages keep a read-only post archive.

The Chrome extension provides the source entry inside X. A post mentioning a token offers a Moon action and post-level statistics. A profile module shows the person's capital connections.

## Participation and rewards

Sources can receive fees from qualifying activity. Relation Tokens record participation and support eligible holder income and access to chats or groups. An application can build on these relationships and define additional experiences. The implementation determines actual eligibility; no fixed return or future trading activity is promised here.

## Delivery

The current product includes a web application and Chrome extension, English and Chinese, appearance preferences and configured mainnet/testnet experiences. Identity and economic relationships are anchored on Monad; configured execution also includes Ethereum. Additional routes or prospective features should be evaluated against their own release status.

The protocol, operational services, detailed mechanisms and complete application are implemented privately. Public excerpts show selected interface presentation. See the implementation-status record for evidence boundaries.
